What I care about

I like building things that make people feel more capable. My favorite work sits somewhere between engineering, product thinking, and writing: understand the problem clearly, make the first version small, and keep improving until it feels obvious.

I am especially interested in tools for learning, research, operations, and personal knowledge work.

Beliefs

  • Simple systems usually win because more people can understand and maintain them.
  • Good taste is built by noticing details, not by memorizing rules.
  • Writing is a design tool. If I cannot explain a feature plainly, I probably do not understand it yet.
  • The best products respect attention and make the next action clear.

Fun facts

  • I keep a running list of useful questions, favorite sentences, and project ideas.
  • I enjoy museums, bookstores, quiet cafes, and unusually good documentation.
  • My default note-taking system is intentionally low-tech: plain text first, tools second.
